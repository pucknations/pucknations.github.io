PUCK NATIONS

Privacy policy

Last updated 6 November 2026

What Puck Nations is

Puck Nations is an automated channel that publishes simulated ice hockey matches between nations to its own accounts on YouTube and Instagram. The "Puck Nations Poster" app is a private tool used only by the Puck Nations owner to upload those videos to the Puck Nations channel.

Data we collect

The app does not collect, store or share personal data about viewers or anyone else. It does not use cookies, analytics or tracking on this website.

Use of YouTube and Google data

The app uses the YouTube API Services with a single permission, youtube.upload, to upload videos to the Puck Nations channel owned by the app's operator. It does not read, store or share any other YouTube or Google account data. Access tokens are kept in encrypted storage and used only to upload videos.

By using a YouTube feature of this project you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove the app's access at any time from the Google security settings page.

Data retention and deletion

The app stores no personal data, so there is nothing to retain or delete. Its only stored credential is the OAuth token for the Puck Nations channel, kept in encrypted secret storage. It is deleted when access is revoked at the Google security settings page, and any remaining copy is removed within 7 days of revocation. To ask about any data, email the address below and we will reply within 30 days.

Instagram

The app uses Meta's Instagram API only to publish Reels to the Puck Nations Instagram account. It does not access other users' data.
